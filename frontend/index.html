Questions? Answers

Short answers to the things people usually ask before signing up.

What happens right after I sign up?

You'll go straight into the NetSentinel dashboard, where you can run scans and see live security alerts. Nothing is installed on your computer.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes 10 scans a month — enough to check a small site. No credit card needed.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Open the landing page, sign up, and use the app. If a firewall block is needed, we'll give you a script to run as Administrator.

What does the scanner actually find?

Common web risks like SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and unsafe login forms — each result comes with a plain-English explanation and a suggested fix.

Can I try it before creating an account?

You can see the demo on this page, but full scans need an account so results are saved and you can open your dashboard.
